LAB 3 · STARTER GAME

Coin Dash — Ariel & Or

Collect yellow coins. Dodge red drones. How many can you collect in 45 seconds?

Changing the level starts a new round.

Move: arrow keys / WASD · Pause: P · Restart: R

Two players: P1 (teal) uses WASD; P2 (orange) uses arrow keys. Most coins wins; a drone ends the round for both.

The purple chaser slowly follows the nearest player. Keep moving to stay ahead!

Collect the blue >> power-up for 5 seconds of extra speed.

Keep clear of the orange pulse enemy: it grows and shrinks, and its expanding edge is dangerous.
